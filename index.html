My First 2 Days of Code

A little reflection on coding, AI, and me.

Day 01

What I learned

My first day at the GirlCode Boot Camp was exciting, informative, and full of new experiences. We started the day by introducing ourselves and getting to know one another. After the introductions, we were introduced to BizTech Nexus, a company where business and technology converge. We learned about the company's background, its purpose, what it does, and the opportunities it creates for young people who want to build careers in technology. This gave me a better understanding of how businesses and technology work together to solve real-world problems.

The next session focused on Artificial Intelligence (AI), which was one of the most interesting parts of the day. We learned how to use AI effectively by writing clear, detailed, and specific prompts. One of the biggest lessons I learned was that every person's prompt can be different because our phones, experiences, and needs are different. Even if two people ask AI the same question, they may receive different responses depending on how they ask. This taught me that learning how to communicate with AI is just as important as learning the technology itself.

We also learned that AI is becoming a valuable part of our everyday lives. Instead of seeing AI as something that will replace people or take away jobs, we were encouraged to learn how to work alongside it. The facilitators explained that becoming familiar with AI and technology will help us remain competitive in the future workplace. This changed the way I think about AI because I now see it as a useful learning partner rather than a threat.

Another fascinating lesson was learning that AI has different models and applications. I was surprised to discover that some online personalities are actually AI-generated models rather than real people. One famous example is Lil Miquela, who looks like a real person but is actually a virtual AI influencer. We also learned that there are AI hosts and virtual presenters that companies use for marketing, customer service, and communication.

Lastly, we learned how to recognise AI-generated images and videos by paying attention to small details that people usually overlook. Understanding these signs made me realise how important digital awareness has become. Overall, my first day completely changed how I view Artificial Intelligence and inspired me to continue learning technology skills for the future.

Day 02

How things clicked

Day two was more practical and a little more challenging than the first day. We started by working with DotPad, where we were introduced to the basics of creating our own websites. This was the first time many of us had written code, and I quickly realised that coding requires patience, concentration, and problem-solving. I personally struggled with some of the activities and did not get everything correct the first time, but I understood that making mistakes are part of the learning journey.

After working with DotPad, we were introduced to HTML, the language used to build websites. Compared to the previous activity, HTML felt much easier to understand, and I enjoyed learning how websites are structured. We also continued using Artificial Intelligence throughout the lesson. We practised writing prompts again, but this time we were challenged to see whether we could produce better results by writing one detailed prompt instead of many separate prompts. This helped me understand that one well-written prompt is often enough to generate exactly what you need.

Using the business ideas we had created as homework on the previous day, we began building our own websites. We learned how to create different pages and connect them together so users could move smoothly from one page to another. We also learned how to change colours, font styles, font sizes, layouts, and create attractive web pages. Another enjoyable part of the lesson was adding animated images that made our websites more interactive. We also designed our websites to match our business ideas.

Towards the end of the day, we created our GitHub accounts. We learned that GitHub is where we will upload, store, manage, and showcase all the coding projects we complete during the boot camp. Creating my GitHub profile made me feel like I was officially becoming part of the technology community. Although the day was challenging, I left feeling much more confident and proud of everything I had learned.
